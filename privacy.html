Whistlepig Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-24. Contact: shengkaihe528@gmail.com.

Summary

Whistlepig collects nothing itself. It contains no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no crash reporters, and no third-party trackers. Your messages and account live on the Matrix homeserver you choose (matrix.org by default), never on our servers — we operate none.

Data handled by the app

  • Account & messages: stored on your device (iOS Keychain for credentials) and on your Matrix homeserver so you can chat. Direct messages use Matrix end-to-end encryption; the homeserver cannot read them.
  • Media & files you send: uploaded to your homeserver as part of normal chat.
  • Diagnostics you choose to share: none — the app has no bug-report upload.

Data shared with third parties

None by the app. Your homeserver operator (e.g. matrix.org) processes your account data under its own policy when you use their service.

Push notifications

The current release does not register for remote push notifications, so no device tokens leave your phone. This section will be updated if that changes.

Account deletion

You can deactivate your Matrix account at any time in Settings → Delete Account.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be posted on this page before they take effect.
