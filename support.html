Whistlepig Support

Whistlepig is a private Matrix chat app for iPhone and iPad, forked from Element X iOS.

Getting started

  • Sign in: use an existing Matrix account, or create a new one on matrix.org from inside the app.
  • Contacts: start a chat with anyone by their Matrix ID (e.g. @alice:matrix.org).

FAQ

Push notifications?
The first release focuses on foreground chat. Background push notifications are on the roadmap and will arrive in a later update.

How do I delete my account?
Settings → Delete Account, then follow the confirmation. This deactivates your Matrix account on your homeserver.

Is my chat private?
Direct chats use Matrix end-to-end encryption. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Source code?
Whistlepig is published under the GNU AGPLv3. Corresponding source is available at the public mirror repository linked in the app's Legal screen.
